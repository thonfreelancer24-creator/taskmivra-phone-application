# TaskMivra Voice v0.5 — TV Diagnostic Results

Date: 2026-08-28

## Status

**EXPERIMENTAL DIAGNOSTIC ONLY — NOT PRODUCTION-RIGHTS-CLEAN.**

The purpose of this run was to determine whether the current TaskMivra-owned target-speaker architecture can solve the TV/competing-speech failure when the interference distribution is sufficiently matched.

The user's recorded TV interference was split into disjoint portions:
- first ~70%: internal R&D training interference only;
- final ~30%: held-out TV evaluation interference.

The held-out portion was never used for optimization.

## Best diagnostic checkpoint

Epoch 7:
- Held-out TV SI-SDR improvement: **+3.21 dB**
- Average correct-profile advantage over wrong profile: **+4.50 dB**
- Training SI-SDR: **3.76 dB**
- Profile-switch penalty: **0.014**

This exceeds the locked +1 dB TV SI-SDRi gate on the disjoint held-out portion and demonstrates that the architecture itself can suppress TV-like interference when trained on sufficiently matched data.

## Important limitation

Some individual profile-switch slices still failed (`min_profile_gap ≈ -2.44 dB`). Therefore this diagnostic checkpoint is not accepted for production even aside from data-rights concerns.

More importantly, because the user's TV recording may contain copyrighted broadcast/program material, these diagnostic weights MUST NOT be treated as the rights-clean TaskMivra production model.

## Engineering conclusion

The previous v0.4 failure was primarily a **training-distribution/data-diversity problem**, not proof that the TaskMivra separator architecture is incapable of TV suppression.

Next production step:
1. obtain/download genuinely different public-domain or explicitly commercial-training-cleared human speakers;
2. create TV-speaker variants from those cleared recordings;
3. retrain from random initialization without the user's TV recording in the training corpus;
4. test the resulting rights-clean checkpoint against the untouched user TV recording;
5. require both TV SI-SDRi >= +1 dB and robust positive profile-switch margin before human listening approval.
