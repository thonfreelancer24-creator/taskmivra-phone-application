## Reproducibility rule for all future candidates
A candidate MUST NOT be labeled final/accepted unless its package includes:
1. exact model checkpoint bytes or a durable artifact reference;
2. SHA-256 of the checkpoint;
3. architecture/source commit SHA;
4. training-manifest hash;
5. inference configuration;
6. tone-shaping configuration;
7. A/B acceptance WAVs and metrics;
8. explicit human-listening decision.

If checkpoint bytes are missing, preserve the listening decision as a tone/reference target only and retrain under a new candidate version. Never claim a rebuilt checkpoint is the same model.
