# TaskMivra Voice v6.4 — Crystal-reference dual specialist

Date: 2026-08-30

## Status

**BUILT / OBJECTIVE VALIDATION COMPLETE — HUMAN LISTENING REQUIRED BEFORE LIVE PHONE INTEGRATION.**

v5.1 was rejected by human listening because audible background remained in every processed entry. v6.4 removes that conservative attenuation floor and uses two TaskMivra-owned real-valued mask specialists while preserving original microphone phase.

## Architecture

- Quiet/clean input: exact PCM bypass.
- Environmental noise: `TaskMivraRealMask-v6`.
- Broadband continuous AC/water/shower: v6 plus TaskMivra-written residual spectral suppression.
- Competing speech / TV-like interference: `TaskMivraRealMask-v6.1`.
- Router: logistic classifier over disagreement between the v6 and v6.1 attenuation masks.
- Both neural models output attenuation magnitude only. They do not synthesize complex phase.
- Frequencies above the learned 0–8 kHz control band remain on the original 48 kHz microphone path.

## Commercial / provenance boundary

- v6 training: TaskMivra/user-cleared clean voices 1–4 plus TaskMivra-owned environmental recordings 7–12.
- v6.1 fine-tuning: deterministic synthetic competing-speaker and TV/speaker-like transforms made only from TaskMivra-owned clean speech, plus owned environmental recordings.
- Router training: TaskMivra-owned environmental mixtures versus deterministic synthetic speaker/TV-like mixtures only.
- `Clean Voice 5` remained held out.
- Real TV and close-music recordings remained held-out evaluation only and were not used for training or parameter selection.
- Locked Crystal Voice v0.4 remains external listening/quality reference only. No benchmark code, weights, generated outputs, distillation targets, or pretrained components are used.

## Fixed router

The mask-disagreement router reached 92.9% validation accuracy on its TaskMivra-only synthetic/owned validation set. The decision threshold is the standard logistic-regression threshold `0.50`; held-out TV/music were not used to choose it.

## Hard human gate

Reject v6.4 if listening reveals any of the following:

- background remains materially audible instead of being suppressed;
- static/crackle;
- broken or chopped syllables;
- metallic/watery/robotic voice;
- thin or distant voice;
- pumping;
- missing consonants or word boundaries.

Do not connect this candidate to TaskMivra Phone media until the human listening gate passes.
